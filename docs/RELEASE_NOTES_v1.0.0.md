## Scientific boundaries

- Source JPEGs are not redistributed.
- The experimental files are JPEG derivatives and are not treated as verified original acquisition DICOM objects.
- Archive grouping does not establish patient independence.
- The regional mask is geometric rather than a clinical ROI.
- The results do not establish universal robustness, clinical preservation, or general steganographic security.
